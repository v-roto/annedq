About Me

Hello, I'm Anne De Quevedo, a concept artist and illustrator specializing in naturalistic fantasy.

My passion is bringing imaginary worlds, fascinating creatures, and memorable characters to life through digital and 3D art. I work exploring nature, light, and visual storytelling using professional tools like Blender, Photoshop, Substance Painter, Procreate, and more.

I am always open to new creative challenges, commissions, and projects in the entertainment, video game, and tabletop RPG industries.
